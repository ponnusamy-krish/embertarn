# V3 image review

Five real photographs were downloaded before iteration work and remain outside public/. They never enter the renderer. Every pass captured explore and cinematic 20/60/120/200/280. Comparison sheets place real Moraine photos beside all six frames.

1. v3-1: Mirrored geography, pale snow blankets, tile seams, blunt cones, green blobs and blue wash. Reflection included mountains but shore followed false plateau contours. Corrected coordinates/extent, used actual lake polygon, rebuilt cameras, added relit normal impostors and shoreline scatter.
2. v3-2: Narrow lake recognizable. References have dense dark spruce and fractured cliffs; render had pale trees, seams and cloud blobs. Fixed boundary normals, skirt shadow artifacts and rock scale; tried photographic dusk HDR.
3. v3-3: Reflections coherent and seams gone. Mauve wash, blank gray sky, broad crowns and uniform bands remained. Reduced ambient, lowered/warmed sun, narrowed/darkened trees, increased density, reduced AO outlines and broke snow by aspect.
4. v3-4: Better valley shadow, but smooth folds and gray sky remained synthetic. Increased mountain mesh detail, added domain-warped fractures, selected cloud-bearing Table Mountain 2 HDR from thumbnail comparisons, raised reflection resolution and added MSAA.
5. v3-5: Stronger relief and cloud realism. Cliffs too uniformly crumpled, north outlet a flat shelf, scanned trees too bright. Reduced displacement, stopped flattening low terrain globally, varied snow and added foreground scanned boulders.
6. v3-6: Better outlet and less noisy crags. Near trees still mismatched and shore lacked plants. Matched near/far color, width and baked visibility; added sedges and bounded collision/LOD searches spatially.
7. v3-7/final: Full-scene reflections, warm exposed ridges, dense shaded forest and a wet margin are present. Against references, synthetic cliff detail, repeated fir silhouettes and even shoreline blending remain visible. Universally photo-indistinguishable frames have not been achieved.

8. v3-8: Curvature-based gully snow adds patches below the upper snow line. Reflection and forest retain their improvements. Snow transitions and synthetic crags remain softer and more repetitive than the photographs; this is recorded as a remaining limitation.
